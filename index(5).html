About us

Skin art, rooted in tradition

Welcome to MilliMehndhi, named after my beloved grandma and my favourite thing to do. Here tradition meets artistry.

My journey began in childhood, inspired by watching other's intricate henna work, leading me to hone my craft from paper sketches to intricate client designs.

Driven by a commitment to quality and wellness, I exclusively use premium organic henna for all bookings, offering custom patterns that bring beauty, balance, and elegance to every occasion.
